Draft analytical Models — not an official district fiscal estimate.

Models 1–3 are analytical scenarios, not forecasts of future district appropriations. Model 1 divides modeled benefits equally among 49 LDs. Model 2 uses demographic/program allocation weights. Model 3 applies a $1.7 standard local multiplier to each Model 2 allocation as an illustration; it is not an additional appropriation. LD14 and LD29 use comparable-district millionaire-count and average-income proxy inputs.

SB 6346 vote badges show each legislator’s final chamber vote; click a badge for the official Washington Legislature roll call.
