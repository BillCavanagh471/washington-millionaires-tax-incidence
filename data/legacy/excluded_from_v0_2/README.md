# Historical LD Millionaire Estimates - Excluded from v0.2

The files in this directory are retained solely for historical provenance and,
where explicitly permitted, post-model benchmarking.

They are prohibited inputs to the de novo millionaire-geography v0.2 model.

In particular, historical legislative-district estimates of millionaire return
counts or millionaire AGI must not be used for:

- predictor selection;
- model fitting;
- coefficient estimation;
- calibration;
- geographic allocation;
- validation;
- model selection; or
- acceptance/rejection of v0.2.

The v0.2 model must be constructed from independently sourced authoritative data.

The historical LD estimates may be examined only after the v0.2 methodology and
results have been independently produced and frozen, and any subsequent comparison
must be clearly identified as a benchmark comparison rather than validation.

This exclusion is intentional and is part of the v0.2 methodology.
